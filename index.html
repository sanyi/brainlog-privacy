Privacy Policy

BrainLog is an open-source, non-commercial voice notes application built with privacy and offline reliability as its core design. We respect your privacy and believe your voice notes belong entirely to you.

1. Data Collection & Storage

BrainLog does not collect, sell, or share your personal data or audio recordings.

  • On-Device Storage: By default, all voice recordings created with BrainLog are stored exclusively on your device's local private storage. The app operates completely offline.
  • Future User-Configured Backends: Future versions of the application may offer optional capabilities allowing you to transmit recordings to a server (for example, for personal cloud backup or remote transcription). Any such functionality will be:
    • Strictly opt-in: Disabled by default unless explicitly enabled by you.
    • User-controlled: Directed exclusively to backend services, endpoints, or self-hosted servers chosen, configured, and managed by you. BrainLog does not operate proprietary backend servers to collect or harvest your audio.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The application does not embed any tracking SDKs, analytics frameworks, advertising trackers, or device fingerprinting tools.
  • Standard Store Telemetry: If installed via Google Play, Google may collect anonymized system diagnostics (e.g., crash stack traces and basic device metadata) as governed by Google's own Privacy Policy.

2. Device Permissions Used

To function as a voice recorder, BrainLog requests the following permissions:

  • RECORD_AUDIO: Needed to capture audio through your device's microphone or connected headset.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE: Required so capture can safely continue even if the screen is locked or you navigate away from the app. A persistent notification is displayed whenever recording is active.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to display the ongoing recording notification and controls.
  • BLUETOOTH_CONNECT: Used on Android 12+ solely to discover and display the friendly name of connected Bluetooth headsets for input routing.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: Used to adjust communication audio routing for headset recording.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

You have complete control over your data. Audio files can be deleted one-by-one from within the app. Uninstalling the app permanently erases all recordings stored in the app's local storage.
